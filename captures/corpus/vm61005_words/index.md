# TI VM61005 words (US male voice), from Talkie

- **Image:** 16376 bytes, SHA-256 `b97dd57cd6657eb46fe513981e440d4d4dbb5ffc96a3a0fd20eabafa203c82c3`, bank 3. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** Talkie (Peter Knight), Vocab_US_Large: words copied from the VM61005, which is not available as an image. Every Talkie word from the PROMs that are held (VM61002, VM61004, VM71003A, CM62024, TI-99) matches its image byte for byte, so these are taken to be verbatim too. They are assembled here into a VSM image in TI's indexed format, in Talkie's order; the layout is not the original's, so only the words are original. The names are Talkie's.
- **Captured:** 116 of 116 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).
