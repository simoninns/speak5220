# Phase 8 test vectors: loc

- **Image:** 628 bytes, SHA-256 `9d68829281e47d334115337b3c487b2cb0da5230872d4670fa2af0818023d29a`, bank 0. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** tools/vectors.py set 'loc': Phase 9: localising the lowest-order error
(docs-tech/lowest_order_arithmetic_ideas.md sections 4.2 and 4.3).
Unvoiced frames (no tails; K5-K10 zero) with two of K1-K4 large at
once, so that an error in one product reaches the output by a
different route from an error in another; K1 = -488 (index 6, the
worst unvoiced K1) at energies with each lowest Booth digit; and loud
frames followed by silence frames, which keep the K and let the filter
ring down freely from a large amplitude to its limit cycle.
- **Captured:** 31 of 31 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).
