- **Captured:** 206 of 206 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).

## Address sweep

- **Captured:** 1983 of the 15861 sweep addresses, each spoken through the VSM (`sweep/addr_<address>/`). [`sweep.csv`](sweep.csv) lists them all.
- **Covered:** every byte address of the image that is not a word start (words are listed above), except the 317 whose data reaches the end of the image without a stop code. Those run on into the next bank, which the Pico serves as empty, so what the chip reads there depends on the board, not on the PROM.
- **Checks:** 1979 of 1983 takes passed every check. Failed takes are kept; addresses with a failed check: layer2_vs_layer3 (4). 4 addresses have no take that passed: 0x005AF, 0x016BD, 0x02B26, 0x02CA3.
- **Parsed:** 1983 ended with a stop code, after a median of 12 frames (at most 136).
