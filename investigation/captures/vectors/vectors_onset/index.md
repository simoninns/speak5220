# Phase 8 test vectors: onset

- **Image:** 1997 bytes, SHA-256 `efeff5f577618d8917ce238ea3d275446ea88ccc4b693717d53bbab844f14425`, bank 0. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** tools/vectors.py set 'onset': Phase 10: the first pitch pulse after silence. In the PROM words the
chip's chirp starts one sample later than the model's after voiced
speech and one or two silence frames, and on time after three or after
unvoiced speech. Voiced, n silence frames, voiced: n from 1 to 6, at
pitches before the silence that leave the pitch counter at different
phases; other pitches after it; and the same after unvoiced speech,
into a repeat frame, at a loud resonant setting, and with no silence.
- **Captured:** 52 of 52 words. Each take directory follows `docs/technical/capture/data-layout.md`; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).
