# TI VM71003A (US female voice, talking clock)

- **Image:** 4096 bytes, SHA-256 `7bc29d27427f7fc945392b98f7051b0520b5fb5c5b06fbe127184d9878928bb8`, bank 4. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** TMS6100 Multi-PHROM Emulator (eRadiofan), ROMdata_VM71003A.c, padded with zeros to 4 KB; the result is MAME's dump. The word names are from its word list. A 4 KB VSM: the Pico serves it padded with zeros to 16 KB, so reads past 4 KB return zeros, where a real 4 KB part might wrap.
- **Captured:** 35 of 35 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).

## Address sweep

- **Captured:** 489 of the 3906 sweep addresses, each spoken through the VSM (`sweep/addr_<address>/`). [`sweep.csv`](sweep.csv) lists them all.
- **Covered:** every byte address of the image that is not a word start (words are listed above), except the 155 whose data reaches the end of the image without a stop code. Those run on into the next bank, which the Pico serves as empty, so what the chip reads there depends on the board, not on the PROM.
- **Checks:** 488 of 492 takes passed every check. Failed takes are kept; addresses with a failed check: start_slot (3), layer2_vs_layer3 (1). 1 addresses have no take that passed: 0x10133.
- **Parsed:** 489 ended with a stop code, after a median of 18 frames (at most 130).
- **Repeats:** 3 addresses have more than one take; 0 of them gave the same output every time (`outputs` in `sweep.csv` counts the distinct outputs).
