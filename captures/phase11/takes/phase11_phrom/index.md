# Phase 11 PHROM: new words, numbers and phrases (Acorn format)

- **Image:** 16384 bytes, SHA-256 `bb9416ed9b3945af760656957907dbc8f9175e00307fc0a2d6b8d8b99d95fd2e`, bank 0. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** tools/phase11.py: the single words, numbers and computer phrases of tools/prompts.csv, one voice each, encoded by encoder/ and laid out as an Acorn PHROM (datasheet section 12.8)
- **Captured:** 58 of 58 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).
