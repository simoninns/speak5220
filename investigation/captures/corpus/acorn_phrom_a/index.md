# Acorn PHROM A (Kenneth Kendall voice)

- **Image:** 16384 bytes, SHA-256 `8c093401661f530032c5aca8fd80d91af58e596d082a51be58ce2ee063a89308`, bank F. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** TMS6100-Emulator (simoninns): a dump of an Acorn PHROM A made with HxD, held in this repo as firmware/src/romdata_acorn.h. The word names are from its word list, whose addresses and numbers all match the image's pointer table. Four of its names contradict the names stored in the image (stored_name) and are corrected from them (listed_as keeps the list's).
- **Captured:** 165 of 165 words. Each take directory follows `docs/technical/capture/data-layout.md`; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).

## Address sweep

- **Captured:** 16144 of the 16144 sweep addresses, each spoken through the VSM (`sweep/addr_<address>/`). [`sweep.csv`](sweep.csv) lists them all.
- **Covered:** every byte address of the image that is not a word start (words are listed above), except the 75 whose data reaches the end of the image without a stop code. Those run on into the next bank, which the Pico serves as empty, so what the chip reads there depends on the board, not on the PROM.
- **Checks:** 17170 of 17266 takes passed every check. Failed takes are kept; addresses with a failed check: start_slot (80), layer2_vs_layer3 (10). 2 addresses have no take that passed: 0x3F40D, 0x3F416.
- **Parsed:** 16144 ended with a stop code, after a median of 18 frames (at most 151).
- **Repeats:** 335 addresses have more than one take; 22 of them gave the same output every time (`outputs` in `sweep.csv` counts the distinct outputs).
