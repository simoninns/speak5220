# Synthetic vectors

The aim is the chip's internal behaviour, measured and written up: the lookup tables, chirp, noise generator, interpolation, lattice filter arithmetic, DAC transfer function, frame format, and VSM and host interface timing. The stimulus is described in [controlled stimulus](../stimulus.md): the Speak External sweeps, the synthetic VSM tests and the timing tests.

## Vectors, model and tests

- **The vectors** (`investigation/tools/vectors.py`): 480 vectors in eight sets (energy, pitch, K, noise, interpolation, special frames, random, loud). Each set is written as a synthetic PROM in `investigation/captures/phase8/vectors/`, so `run.py` captures it like any other through both paths. All were captured twice per path with the standard procedure: 1936 takes (480 vectors × 2 takes × 2 paths, plus 16 retakes), about 2 GB, kept local in `investigation/captures/vectors/` with their index files committed ([data layout](../data-layout.md#where-it-is-stored)).
- **The model** (`investigation/tools/model.py`): the synthesis path as the datasheet describes it ([§8.9](../../datasheet/reference-algorithm.md)), with options for every alternative tested. `vecfit.py` compares every take with it and measures the tables (`investigation/captures/phase8/analysis/`). The model is the measuring tool behind the text, not the emulator; the [reference emulator](reference-emulator.md) is written from the text and does not copy it ([from captures to the datasheet](../analysis.md#the-model-and-the-emulator)).
- **The host interface and VSM tests** (`investigation/tools/protocol.py`), from the Pico's trace, with no scope: FIFO filling and emptying, the start of Speak External, commands written during speech, Reset bytes during Speak External, and the VSM commands (`investigation/captures/phase8/protocol/`, p8-19 to p8-25).
- **Two bench experiments** (`investigation/captures/phase8/scripts/experiments.py`): the Speak timed 1–3 slots off the standard time, and a loud vector after different predecessors.
- **Evidence:** p8-01 to p8-18, copied with their raw data into `investigation/captures/phase8/evidence/` ([evidence index](../../datasheet/evidence.md)).
- **Tools:** `lpc.encodeFrames` writes frames back to bits; it reproduces every Acorn word to its stop code. `run.py` records where a set's manifest is, picks a calibration word with frames, and resets before locating the frame clock. `process.py` finds the image of a synthetic set from its manifest.

The results are written up in the datasheet's [§3](../../datasheet/cpu-interface.md), [§4](../../datasheet/vsm-interface.md), [§5](../../datasheet/io-structure.md), [§6](../../datasheet/commands.md), [§7](../../datasheet/power-up.md) (noise generator, previous utterance, upsets), [§8.1](../../datasheet/coded-parameters.md), [§8.5](../../datasheet/frame-timing.md), [§8.6 and §8.7](../../datasheet/excitation-interpolation.md), [§8.8](../../datasheet/lattice-filter.md) and [§8.9](../../datasheet/reference-algorithm.md), with [§2](../../datasheet/clock.md) and [§11.2](../../datasheet/pins.md) completed. The lattice filter's section states two things the vectors could not settle: its lowest-order arithmetic and its overflow. The model is one code off for voiced frames at about one sample per pitch period ([reference emulator validation](reference-emulator.md)).

## Findings

- **The tables.** All 14 energy entries and all 63 pitch entries were confirmed as unique fits, and 142 of 168 K entries; no entry was contradicted. The pitch period is the pitch value plus one sample, at every index.
- **The multiplier is the patent's.** Every product, the energy's included, goes through a Booth array whose stage 0 inverts without the +1 and whose result has its LSB forced to 1 ([US 4,335,277](../../sources/patent-us4335277/index.md)). That cut the error on voiced vectors 4–5 times and explains why the idle code is −1.
- **The noise register is fully determined.** 13 bits, feedback from bits 12, 3, 2 and 0, one step per slot, sign from bit 0. It matched 32,699 samples bit for bit. Its state at the first sample is a fixed number of steps after 0x1FFF, counted from the command: shifting the Speak by 1–3 slots shifts the state by 1–3 steps, through both paths.
- **Timing inside a frame.** The frame read at sample 0 becomes the target 25 samples later. Each parameter reaches the filter at a fixed sample of its interpolation period: energy at 2, pitch at 5, K*j* at 2*j*+3. The voiced/unvoiced switch acts 2 samples after the latch.
- **The previous utterance.** A Speak does not clear the chip's stored parameters; it only hides them from the filter until the first frame takes effect. An utterance that starts with a repeat frame, as the [Acorn PHROM A](acorn-phrom-a.md#findings) bench target does, uses the previous utterance's K values. Words start with full frames and so do not show it. What is carried is the coded parameters, not the interpolator's registers ([reference emulator validation](reference-emulator.md#what-the-emulator-found-unclear-in-the-text), [datasheet §7](../../datasheet/power-up.md)).
- **Interpolation and zeroing rules.** Interpolation from unvoiced to a stop frame is inhibited. A voiced repeat frame keeps K5–K10 zeroed. K5–K10 zeroing waits, like other inhibited changes, for IP 0. After speech ends, the filter keeps running while its parameters are zeroed one per PC.
- **The FIFO.** Talk Status sets at the 9th byte; shorter streams never start. Buffer Low sets at 8 bytes or fewer. A frame that cannot be read in full sets Buffer Empty, and ends the speech like a stop frame, a frame later. That corrects the original, which says at once. Buffer Empty raises no INT, which also corrects the original.
- **Commands during speech.** Load Address, Read Byte and Read and Branch are held until speech ends; Speak is ignored; Reset acts at once. A second command while one is held blocks READY.
- **The reset sequence cannot end a Speak External.** Its bytes become FIFO data. It ends the speech within about two frames instead.
- **Upsets occur in synthetic data too,** at recurring sample positions, and not according to the predecessor.

## Followed up elsewhere

- **The model's remaining one-code differences:** in voiced frames, at a fixed point of each pitch period; and the start rule for a leading silence frame. Overflow is modelled as a 14-bit wrap (US 4,335,277). The voiced error is a different low-order limit cycle in the zero-excitation part of each pitch period, and many arithmetic forms are ruled out ([datasheet §8.8](../../datasheet/lattice-filter.md)). A vector set built to isolate it, voiced frames with one K non-zero at a time, with the longest pitch and a constant energy, so that each period ends in a long free ringing of one lattice stage, is the `ring` set ([reference emulator validation](reference-emulator.md)).
- **Upsets** need bench changes (temperature, supply, decoupling, a second chip); they cannot be made from the host ([known gaps](../../datasheet/gaps.md#upsets-and-chip-specific-departures)).
- **The DAC model for very loud data** ([datasheet §8.2](../../datasheet/dac-and-audio.md#82-da-conversion)) **and the extra DAC noise during speech** ([known gaps](../../datasheet/gaps.md#noise-during-speech)) affect only layer 3.
- **Slots and TI's bit times** remain an inference from the pin name ([known gaps](../../datasheet/gaps.md#slots-and-tis-bit-times)).
- **Host interface tests that US 4,335,277 suggests:** [known gaps](../../datasheet/gaps.md#host-interface-timing).
