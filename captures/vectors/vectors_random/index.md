# Phase 8 test vectors: random

- **Image:** 2804 bytes, SHA-256 `aa3c051f494b4cb8dbf2472d4dc684e8da416dac8cd5da9542ea575d2e8c5cf5`, bank 0. Target chip: TMS5220.
- **Provenance:** tools/vectors.py set 'random': Random frames with every field drawn uniformly (energy 1-14), so that
parameter combinations no encoder would write are covered.
- **Captured:** 60 of 60 words. Each take directory follows `docs-tech/speech_capture_plan.md` §6; its `capture.json` holds the metadata and checks. [`takes.csv`](takes.csv) lists every take, with the SHA-256 of its output (`talk_sha256`: the I/O codes from sample 0 to the end of talk, as 16-bit little-endian integers) and of its raw files.
- **Columns:** Frames and Bits are parsed from what the chip read (VSM) or was sent (Speak External), up to the stop code. ms is the talk time of the first take, from `SCOPE_TRIG` rising to Talk Status clearing. Repeats compares every take with the first, sample for sample, after aligning them. For takes made with the standard procedure, it gives how many match the per-sample majority of all of them exactly, and the samples where no majority exists (`compare.json`).
